Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

SurveyDXF is a field productivity tool for Android that operates entirely on your device. No personal or survey data is collected, transmitted, or stored on any external server.

Data stored on your device

All app data stays on your device and is never sent anywhere automatically:

  • Survey data — DXF drawings, project files, station coordinates, captured points, level-book entries, and settings are stored locally in the app's private storage.
  • License state — your trial start date and purchase status are stored locally. License verification uses Google Play Billing on-device APIs only.
  • Crash reports — if the app closes unexpectedly, a local crash log is saved to your device. This log contains the app version, Android version, device model, and stack trace. It is never sent automatically. You choose whether to share it manually via email or messaging.

What the app does not collect

SurveyDXF does not collect, transmit, or store on any server:

  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Your name, email address, or contact information
  • Your location (GPS or network-based)
  • Your DXF files or survey data
  • Usage analytics, screen views, or telemetry
  • SIM card, network, or locale information

Internet access

The app requests the INTERNET permission solely for Google Play Billing — to verify your license, process purchases, and restore them on a new device. All billing communication is handled by Google Play Services on your device. SurveyDXF does not make network requests to any third-party server.

Payment processing

All payments and subscriptions are processed by Google Play Billing. SurveyDXF does not receive or store your payment card details, billing address, or Google account password. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment transaction.

Data you choose to share

You may export survey data as CSV or PDF files and share them using any app on your device (e.g., email, WhatsApp, cloud storage). These exports are initiated by you and SurveyDXF has no access to them after sharing. You may also manually share a crash report for support purposes.

Children

SurveyDXF is a professional surveying tool not intended for children under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated. Changes will be posted on this page and reflected in the app's Play Store listing.
